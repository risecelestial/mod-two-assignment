Book Recommendations

Fantasy Adventure

The Hobbit

By J. R. R. Tolkien

Bilbo Baggins leaves his comfortable home for an unexpected journey with a group of dwarves.

Good for: Readers who enjoy quests, unusual creatures, and brave characters.

Why read it: It introduces readers to an imaginative fantasy world.

Classic Romance

Pride and Prejudice

By Jane Austen

Elizabeth Bennet navigates family expectations, first impressions, and complicated relationships.

Good for: Readers who enjoy witty dialogue, social observations, and romance.

Why read it: Its memorable characters make it a lasting classic.

Science Fiction

A Wrinkle in Time

By Madeleine L'Engle

Meg Murry travels across space and time to help find her missing father.

Good for: Readers who like imaginative science fiction and stories about courage.

Why read it: It combines a space adventure with themes of family and individuality.
